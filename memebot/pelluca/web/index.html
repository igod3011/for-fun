No. Pelluca shows on-chain facts and our own base rates. "No major flags found" does not mean safe.

Do I need to connect a wallet?

Never. Pelluca is read-only. Anyone asking for your seed phrase in our name is a scammer.

The Solana blockchain and DexScreener. Every number can be checked on a block explorer.
